Transcriptions & translations

Papers I have read closely and typed up in LaTeX — some of them scans with no machine-readable source, some translated from another language. Reading a paper this way is slow, and I have never found a better way to actually understand one.

All credit to the original authors; all errors are mine. These are unofficial typesettings made for my own study, not authorised editions. Where a paper is under copyright, what I post is a transcription for personal and scholarly use — if you are an author or rights-holder and would like something removed, please email me and I will take it down.

Every entry offers the compiled PDF and the LaTeX source. The source is there so you can lift a definition, fix an error, or build on the file — corrections are welcome.
