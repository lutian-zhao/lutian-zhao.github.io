I work in algebraic geometry. My interests include moduli of sheaves, perverse sheaves, and moduli of parabolic Higgs bundles.

Background

Before coming to Tokyo I was a Serguei Novikov Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of Maryland, College Park, where my mentor was Amin Gholampour.

I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from the University of Illinois at Urbana–Champaign under the supervision of Sheldon Katz, and my bachelor's degree in mathematics from Zhiyuan College, Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

Research

Broadly, I study moduli spaces attached to curves and surfaces and the cohomological invariants they carry. Recent work has centred on the tame parahoric nonabelian Hodge correspondence, integrable systems arising from parabolic and parahoric Higgs bundles, and Gopakumar–Vafa invariants computed through perverse sheaves.
